You can run your very own Cloud Invoice on **Cloudflare**, **Vercel** or
**Netlify**; just click a button. No coding, no manual forking and no
command line required.

## What happens when you click

It's easier than it looks. A button does the whole job for you:

1. You sign in with your GitHub account.
2. It makes a **copy of the app's code inside your own account**.
3. It connects that copy to the platform and builds the app.
4. You type in a few small settings (see below).
5. Your own Cloud Invoice is live, usually in a few minutes.

You own the copy, so nothing about your data or your copy is shared with
anyone else. And whenever you change your copy, the platform updates your
site automatically.

## What you need before you click

Only two things:

1. **A GitHub account**. Create one free in a minute if you do not have it.
2. **A free database**. We recommend [Neon](https://neon.tech) or
   [Supabase](https://supabase.com). Create a database and copy its connection
   string. It looks like
   `postgresql://user:password@host:5432/database`.

Every other setting is a nice-to-have. You can always add more later.

## The 3 settings

While deploying, you will be asked for these-

| Setting        | What it is                      | Where to find it                                                                                                                              |
| -------------- | ------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `DATABASE_URL` | Your database connection string | From your database provider                                                                                                                   |
| `AUTH_SECRET`  | A random password for the app   | you can either generate one by this command `openssl rand -base64 32` or from [here](https://www.convertsimple.com/random-base64-generator/). |

<Callout type="info">
  `AUTH_SECRET` is a secret. Never share it and never paste it into an online
  tool. The deploy button only asks you to type it in. It is never stored in the
  code.
</Callout>

## Pick a platform

All three work the same way afterwards; you just choose whichever you like.

<Tabs items={["Cloudflare", "Vercel", "Netlify"]}>
  <Tab value="Cloudflare">
    1. Click **Deploy to Cloudflare** and sign in when asked.
    2. Keep the suggested Worker name the button gives you.
    3. Accept the build settings and let it deploy.

    After the first deploy, open your Worker in the Cloudflare dashboard,
    find **Settings**, paste in the 3 settings above and redeploy once.

  </Tab>
  <Tab value="Vercel">
    1. Click **Deploy with Vercel** and sign in when asked.
    2. Vercel asks you for the 3 settings right away. Type them in.
    3. Click **Deploy** and wait a few minutes.

    Your site is ready. That's it.

  </Tab>
  <Tab value="Netlify">
    1. Click **Deploy to Netlify** and sign in when asked.
    2. Netlify shows you the 3 settings as part of the setup. Fill them in.
    3. Click **Deploy site** and wait a few minutes.

    Your site is ready. That's it.

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## After your first deploy

1. Open your new site's link. It takes you straight to the dashboard.
2. Create your first login. The app has no sign-up page, so create a user by
   running this once on your computer (paste your own database URL and a
   strong password)-

   ```bash
   DATABASE_URL="postgresql://user:password@host:5432/database" \
   SEED_USER_NAME="Admin" SEED_USER_EMAIL="you@example.com" \
   SEED_USER_PASSWORD="choose-a-strong-password" npm run seed
   ```

   (Copy your GitHub copy to your computer first with
   `git clone` if you do not have the code there yet.)

3. Sign in with that email and password and start invoicing.

Stuck? Open an [issue](https://github.com/nexausm/ci/issues) and we will
be happy to help.
