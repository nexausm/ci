# Cloud Invoice

Open-source, self-hostable invoice manager. Create invoices, manage clients, products and track every payment from one clean dashboard with your
data fully under your control.

## Why Cloud Invoice?

- **No subscriptions, no tracking.** The project is AGPL-3.0 licensed, free to
  self-host and never phones home.
- **Your data, your hardware.** The whole codebase is open, so you can inspect
  it, audit it and deploy it anywhere you already run services like Docker, a
  VPS or your own laptop.
- **Completely private.** Cloud Invoice will never
  advertise inside your invoices.

## Features

<Accordions type="single">
  <Accordion title="Invoices">
    Build an invoice with line items, discounts, credits, tax and adjustments,
    mark it sent and keep a permanent record of every document. Supports
    installment schedules and external costs. You set the invoice number.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Track payments">
    See the full lifecycle of each invoice. Draft, sent, partially paid, paid
    and overdue; with monthly received and outstanding totals at a glance.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Clients">
    Maintain your client list and their contact details so billing stays
    consistent and repeatable.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Product catalogue">
    Catalogue what you sell, in BDT and USD, at base and discounted prices, and
    reuse it across invoices.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Company profile">
    Configure your company name, contact details and logo; rendered on every
    invoice.
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>

## Stack

Built with **Next.js 16**, **React 19**, **Prisma 7** and **PostgreSQL**.
Auth is handled by **Auth.js (NextAuth v5)**. The docs site runs on
**FumaDocs**.
